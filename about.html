About North Star Bakery

Our Story

North Star Bakery is a small neighborhood bakery built on a love of handcrafted baking. Our day starts early, with every loaf, roll, and pastry mixed, shaped, and baked in our own kitchen. We keep our menu simple, we bake in small batches, and we take pride in putting honest, well-made baked goods in the hands of the people who live and work nearby.

Our Sourcing Philosophy

We believe good baking does not need a long ingredient list. We choose our ingredients the way we bake: simply and carefully. We buy flour, butter, eggs, and produce from suppliers we trust, and we prefer local and regional sources whenever they are available. Every ingredient is chosen for quality first, because better ingredients make better bread. If it is in our case, it was baked that day.

Staff Highlights

Our small team keeps the bakery running from the first mix of the morning to the last customer of the day. Our bakers shape every loaf by hand, our pastry cooks keep the case full, and our counter staff greet every customer at the door. Together, they make North Star Bakery feel like the neighborhood spot it is.

Our Community

We are proud to be a neighborhood bakery. Whether you are a regular stopping in for your usual or a first-time visitor picking up something new, we are glad to have you. Our goal is simple: to be the friendly, familiar bakery our community can count on, one good bake at a time.
